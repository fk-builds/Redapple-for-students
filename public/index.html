What is the Student
Partner Program?

The Student Partner program teaches students how to identify businesses that may need digital services and how to approach them professionally. You find the right opportunities, we handle the rest.

Find → Approach → Qualify → Submit → Connect

  • Submit qualified leads

  • Verify leads

  • Deliver & manage the project

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What is the Red Apple Student Partner program? ▼
The Student Partner program is a training and opportunity program where students learn client-acquisition skills, find businesses that need digital services, and submit qualified leads to Red Apple Digital Agency.
Do I need to know web development? ▼
No. You only need to find and qualify opportunities. Our team handles development, design, and delivery.
Is commission guaranteed? ▼
Commission is only for eligible successful deals that are verified and closed by our sales team.
How do I find clients? ▼
You will learn to use Google Maps, social media, local directories, and other discovery methods taught in the training modules.
Is there a payment gateway? ▼
No. Commissions are tracked on the platform and paid manually by Red Apple after verification.
Can I track my leads? ▼
Yes. Your dashboard shows lead status, verification, sales progress, and commission status for every submission.
